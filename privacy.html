Application · the “+ 1”

The private balance

A payment between two Counterseal accounts is not published to any public chain. This is the “+ 1” in Blockchain² + 1.

Payments the public cannot see.

On a public chain, anyone can read who paid whom, how much and when, forever. A payment between two Counterseal accounts is an entry in the Counterseal Ledger. It is never published to a public chain. This is the kind of shielding that Railgun made familiar on Ethereum, offered for assets from any supported chain, Bitcoin included.

Privacy systems on public chains
(Railgun, Zcash)		Counterseal Ledger
How privacy is achievedEvery payment is published in encrypted form, with a mathematical proof that it is valid.The payment is not published at all. Only deposits and withdrawals touch a public chain.
Work on your deviceYour phone or PC computes a heavy proof for each payment. Most hardware wallets cannot do it.No proof to compute. Your device shows the payment and takes your confirmation.
Cost per paymentA public-chain transaction fee every time.No public-chain fee for payments inside the Ledger.
ChainsEach system works on its own chain or chain family.One ledger for assets from many chains, Bitcoin included.

What we do and do not claim. "Private" here means private from the public. In the first release, the Ledger's own open-source code reads balances and payment details in order to check them. Stricter levels follow in later releases: first, balances and history that can be read only inside your own Counterseal Token, and then a level with the same reach as Railgun's privacy.

What stops stolen funds from entering.

Funds entering the Ledger must come with a proof that they do not originate from addresses on public lists of thefts and sanctioned sources.

The proof is produced on the owner's side and reveals nothing else about the owner's history. No company examines or identifies depositors.

Stricter privacy levels are planned for later releases.
