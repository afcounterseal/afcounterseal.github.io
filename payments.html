Payments for commerce and for software agents

A stablecoin payment application that runs on the Counterseal wallet. The owner sets the limits. The device shows them.

What it does.

Open standard

Built for x402

x402 is an open standard that lets a web service ask for payment in a stablecoin as part of an ordinary web request. The application is designed to settle x402 payments.

Payer privacy

The settlement service cannot tell which account paid

The design follows the architecture of GNU Taler, a payment system built so that the party settling a payment cannot link it to the payer.

Software agents

Limits an agent cannot exceed

An AI agent can be given a budget, a list of permitted sellers and a time limit. The service enforces the limits and your device shows them. The agent never holds the authority to change them.

How it uses the wallet.

  1. You set aside fundsYou move a stablecoin amount from your wallet into the payment application and confirm it on your device.
  2. You set the limitsFor yourself or for an agent: how much, to whom, and until when. The device shows the limits before you confirm them.
  3. Payments run inside the limitsEach payment is checked by the service against the limits. Nothing outside them is paid.
  4. You can stop itYou can lower a limit or withdraw the remaining funds at any time, confirmed on your device.

Status. The payment application is planned for Release 2.0 and is not built. It is a separate service from the wallet. How it is governed and operated is being decided separately, and may differ from the wallet's arrangement.
